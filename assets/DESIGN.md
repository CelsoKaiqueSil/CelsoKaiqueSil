# Direção visual

Identidade tecnológica escura com ondas de dados em ciano, conexões analíticas
e lupa. Inspirada nas referências visuais escolhidas pelo autor; sem copiar
marcas ou publicar dados de clientes. Cabeçalho e quatro capas ilustrativas
criados com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens.

As capas não são capturas de aplicações nem gráficos de resultados. As prévias
analíticas reais das amostras sintéticas continuam identificadas nos READMEs.

## Arquivos

- `assets/header.png`: cabeçalho do perfil.
- `assets/projects/`: quatro capas dos projetos.
- Cada projeto mantém sua cópia em `docs/images/cover.png`.

## Prompts

### visual_prompt

```text
Create an original premium GitHub profile banner for a Brazilian data analyst, BI and automation portfolio. Wide horizontal banner, aspect ratio 3:1, designed to remain legible at 850 pixels wide. Inspired by two visual directions: flowing three-dimensional cyan dotted data waves on a deep midnight backdrop; and precise connected analytical graphs with an illuminated magnifying glass. Combine these into one coherent sophisticated technology visual. Do not reproduce any existing logo or brand. Background nearly black midnight indigo; luminous cyan and electric blue as dominant accents, a small restrained violet highlight in the data-wave edges. High quality 3D data particles forming a smooth flowing wire-mesh terrain across the bottom third, connected data nodes and thin analytical lines on the right. On the far right a clean translucent glass magnifying lens reveals a small crisp bar chart and line chart, integrated into the point network. Keep the left two-thirds dark and uncluttered for impeccable typography, with comfortable margins. No robot hand, no person, no floating UI panels, no random numbers, no extra fake interface labels, no stock watermark, no border, no decorative card. Render the exact Portuguese text with correct accents in a modern strong sans-serif, left aligned: large primary line "CELSO KAIQUE"; smaller secondary line "DADOS · BI · AUTOMAÇÃO"; small restrained line below "Python  /  SQL  /  Power BI  /  Excel". White primary typography, cyan secondary typography, pale cool grey stack. Name is the strongest visual element, substantial and readable, with lots of breathing space. Professional, memorable, crisp and atmospheric, not a generic corporate green infographic. All text must be exact and no other text may appear.
```
